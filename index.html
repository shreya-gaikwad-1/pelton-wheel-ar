The wheel, in your room

Place the Pelton wheel on a real table at life size and walk around it.

  1. Tap "View in your room".
  2. Move your phone slowly over a table or the floor until the wheel appears.
  3. Walk around it to see the nozzle, buckets and shaft from every side.

Works on Android phones with Google Play Services for AR, and on iPhone or iPad with iOS 12 or later. If the button does not show, your device does not support AR; open this page on a phone.
